LEVEL 07 — “Greed”
Purpose
Punish rushing
Force form-switching
Make every platform meaningful
Create escalating tension without randomness
Visual First Impression (Deception)
Tall vertical shaft
Clean, evenly spaced platforms
Door clearly visible at the top
Looks easier than previous levels
This is intentional.

Layout (Simple, No Decoration)
6 horizontal platforms stacked vertically
All platforms are usable — none are fake
Each platform spans ~60–70% of screen width
Door is just above the top platform
No spikes visible at first.

Hidden System: Greed Meter (Invisible)
The level tracks how aggressively the player plays, using three triggers:
Jump frequency
Time between landings
Form abuse
The player is never told this.

Platform Mechanics (Every Platform Has a Job)
Platform 1–2 (Bottom)
Completely safe
Teaches nothing new
Lets the player feel confident
This is bait.
Platform 3 — The First Tax

Trigger:
Two Triangle jumps in quick succession

Effect:
Gravity increases slightly
Player doesn’t notice yet
If player uses Circle here:
Screen shrink timer quietly starts

Platform 4 — The Warning
Trigger:
Fast upward movement (Circle abuse)
Or rapid platform skipping

Effect:
A delayed spike rises from the platform just left
Spike activates after the player leaves, not on landing

If player waits:
No spike
But Circle shrink begins if idle too long

Platform 5 — The Trap
This is the key platform.

Behavior:
Platform subtly slides horizontally after landing
If Triangle is active:
Gravity increase becomes noticeable
If Square is active:
Speed decay starts to matter
Rushing causes:
Missed alignment
Awkward jump angles
Panic

Platform 6 — The Punishment
Trigger:
Player tries to brute-force with Triangle jumps

Effect:
Gravity ramps so high that:
Jump height decreases mid-air
Player slams back down
Spike wall slowly rises from below
No instant death.
Just pressure.

Door Behavior (Final Betrayal)
Door moves upward slowly
The faster the player reaches it:
The faster it moves away
If player calms down:
Door stabilizes
